Open Positions

PhD students

If you are interested in joining the lab, please apply through one of the following programs:

  • Florida Atlantic University's Neuroscience Graduate Program
  • The IMPRS program at Max Planck Florida Institute for Neuroscience
  • The Integrative Biology program at Florida Atlantic University

We are looking for students with a solid quantitative background (basic knowledge about linear algebra, differential equations, probability and statistics, single and multivariable calculus, and programming) and a strong interest in understanding the brain (you don't need to have prior research experience in neuroscience or a degree in neuroscience). Feel free to express your interest prior to applying by sending me an email (yueliu@fau.edu).

Postdocs

We are looking for postdocs with a PhD degree in neuroscience, physics, mathematics, computer science, or a related field, preferably with research experience in computational/theoretical neuroscience. If you are interested, please send me an email (yueliu@fau.edu) with your CV and a brief description of your past research and future research interests.

Undergraduate and master students

If you are interested in doing research in the lab, please reach out to me (yueliu@fau.edu)
